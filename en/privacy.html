Privacy

Bike Auto Unlock · Updated 17 October 2026

Your data on this iPhone

Your lock credential is stored in this iPhone’s Keychain. Settings, battery readings and diagnostic events stay local. The app does not request your location and contains no advertising or analytics features.

Signing in to BLACK X

Sign-in sends your account and a derived password value directly to BLACK X over HTTPS. The app does not save your password. BLACK X handles the request under its own privacy policy. After setup, release happens locally over Bluetooth.

Sharing diagnostics and removing data

Diagnostic data leaves your iPhone only when you choose to share it. It contains times, states and signal values, not account details or keys. Remove lock from this iPhone deletes local access and monitoring. Delete diagnostic events separately in Diagnostics. Your BLACK X account remains unchanged.

Privacy

Battery alerts are local notifications. You can disable them in Settings. These alerts do not use a push server.
